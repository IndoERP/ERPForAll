    Complete Accounting Menus, Configuration, and Reports Reference

    This reference catalogs every menu, ledger tool, configuration table, and financial report in the ERPForAll Accounting application so accountants, controllers, and administrators can locate any screen immediately.

    Dashboard, Customers (AR), and Vendors (AP)

    Section titled “Dashboard, Customers (AR), and Vendors (AP)”
    Menu PathPurposeTypical Role
    Accounting → DashboardJournal cards summarizing open Customer Invoices, Vendor Bills, Bank, and Cash balances with quick actionsAccounting Read-Only+
    Customers → InvoicesDraft, post, print, and settle customer invoices (INV/...)Billing / Accountant
    Customers → PaymentsRecord standalone inbound customer receipts or inspect payments linked to invoices (PBNK1/...)Billing / Accountant
    Customers → Batch PaymentsGroup multiple customer receipts from one journal into a single deposit batch for bank reconciliationAccountant
    Customers → Credit NotesIssue partial or full customer invoice reversals (RINV/...)Billing / Accountant
    Customers → Follow-up ReportsTrack overdue customer accounts grouped by Follow-up Level for collectionsAccountant
    Vendors → BillsRecord, 3-way match, and post supplier bills (BILL/...)Billing / Accountant
    Vendors → PaymentsRecord outgoing supplier payments or inspect payments linked to vendor billsBilling / Accountant
    Vendors → Batch PaymentsCombine multiple vendor payments into a single bank payment batchAccountant
    Vendors → RefundsRecord supplier credit notes and bill reversals (RBILL/...)Billing / Accountant

    Accounting Operations (Accounting → More → Accounting)

    Section titled “Accounting Operations (Accounting → More → Accounting)”
    Menu PathFeature Toggle / ScopePurpose
    Journal EntriesCore AccountingCreate, post, and reverse manual or automated double-entry moves (GEN/..., INV/..., BILL/..., BNK1/...)
    Journal ItemsCore AccountingGranular line-level ledger register (account.move.line) with filtering by account, partner, date, tax, and reconciliation
    Bank StatementsCore AccountingImport CSV or manually enter bank/cash statements with starting, computed, and ending balances
    Bank ReconciliationCore AccountingQueue of unreconciled bank lines (To Reconcile) matched against open AR/AP items or counterpart accounts
    Analytic ItemsControlling toggleIndividual cost/revenue postings tagged to Cost Centers, Profit Centers, or Projects
    Cost Allocation RunsControlling toggleExecute and post periodic SKF or percentage overhead assessments between sender and receiver cost centers
    BudgetsBudgeting & AVC toggleMaintain fiscal budgets, planned lines, approved revisions, and live AVC balances (Released, Committed, Actual, Available)
    Budget AmendmentsBudgeting & AVC toggleAuditable workflow for mid-year budget supplements, returns, or transfers (Draft → Approved & Applied)
    Budget CommitmentsBudgeting & AVC toggleView active encumbrances created by confirmed Purchase Orders consuming budget availability prior to billing
    Assets & CIPFixed Assets & CIP toggleRegister depreciable Fixed Assets and Construction in Progress (CIP) projects, compute depreciation boards, and post entries
    CIP SettlementsFixed Assets & CIP toggleCapitalize accumulated CIP costs (partial or full settlement) into commissioned Fixed Assets with automatic journal entries
    Depreciation RunsFixed Assets & CIP toggleBatch-post all due depreciation lines across running assets up to a chosen cutoff date in one run
    Recurring & DeferredCore AccountingManage recurring journal templates, prepaid expense amortization, and deferred revenue recognition schedules

    Multi-Company Consolidation (Accounting → More → Consolidation)

    Section titled “Multi-Company Consolidation (Accounting → More → Consolidation)”

    Available when Group Consolidation is enabled in Accounting Settings:

    Menu PathPurpose
    Consolidation GroupsDefine parent/subsidiary scopes, ownership %, consolidation method (Full, Proportional, Equity), and bootstrap/map the Group FS Chart
    Consolidation RunsExecute period group consolidation with automatic FX translation (CTA), IC elimination, NCI separation, and $0.00 balance check
    Consolidated Balance SheetGroup Balance Sheet after FX translation, IC eliminations, and NCI (set Period to This year or run range)
    Consolidated Profit & LossGroup Profit & Loss rolling up revenues, expenses, and net profit attributable to parent and NCI
    IC Reconciliation MatrixCross-entity matrix comparing intercompany balances between every pair of group subsidiaries

    Complete Accounting Configuration (Accounting → More → Configuration)

    Section titled “Complete Accounting Configuration (Accounting → More → Configuration)”
    Menu PathPurpose
    SettingsEnable/disable enterprise toggles (Controlling, Budgeting & AVC, Fixed Assets & CIP, Group Consolidation), lock dates, default taxes, and currencies
    Chart of AccountsDefine ledger account codes, account types, currencies, and reconciliation flags
    Account TagsTag accounts for Cash Flow classification, tax grids, or custom dynamic report expressions
    JournalsConfigure Sales, Purchase, Bank, Cash, and Miscellaneous journals with default accounts and sequences
    Fiscal PeriodsOpen, review, and close accounting periods by fiscal year
    LedgersConfigure statutory and parallel management reporting ledgers (for example, Management Reporting)
    Taxes & Tax groupsMaintain percentage/fixed VAT, withholding, and sales/purchase tax rules and invoice display groupings
    Fiscal PositionsMap domestic taxes and accounts to export, tax-exempt, or regional equivalents
    Payment TermsConfigure due-date rules and multi-installment schedules
    Follow-up LevelsConfigure overdue reminder stages (for example, First Reminder 15d, Second Reminder 30d, Final Notice 60d)
    Cash RoundingsDefine invoice/cash rounding precision, strategy, and rounding profit/loss accounts
    IncotermsStandard international trade delivery terms (EXW, FCA, FOB, CIF, DDP, etc.) shown on invoices and orders
    Asset ClassesDefine Fixed Asset / CIP categories with default asset, accumulated depreciation, and expense accounts plus depreciation method
    Analytic PlansDefine multi-dimensional analytic axes (for example, Departments, Projects)
    Budget Control ProfilesDefine AVC warning and hard-stop percentage rules (for example, 80% Warn / 100% Block)
    Cost & Profit CentersMaintain departmental cost centers and revenue-bearing profit centers
    Default Cost Center RulesAutomatically derive default analytic/cost center splits by account prefix, partner, or product
    Statistical Key FiguresMaintain non-financial allocation drivers (for example, Employee Headcount (FTE) or floor area)
    Cost Allocation CyclesDefine recurring overhead assessment/distribution cycles mapping sender cost centers to receivers via SKF or fixed shares

    See the worked examples in Accounting Full-Cycle Example and Budget Control (AVC), Controlling & Depreciation Full-Cycle Example to walk through these screens step by step.
