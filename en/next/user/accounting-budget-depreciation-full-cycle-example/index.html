    Budget Control (AVC), Controlling & Fixed Asset Depreciation Full-Cycle Example

    This worked tutorial walks through three enterprise finance cycles inside ERPForAll Accounting:

    1. Budget Control (AVC): From activating a $1,000.00 budget under a Strict Hard-Stop profile (80% Warn / 100% Block), crossing 85% (Warning), hitting a live >100% Hard-Stop Block, approving a +$500.00 Budget Amendment, and successfully posting the blocked bill.
    2. Fixed Asset Depreciation & CIP: Posting Period 1 straight-line depreciation on a $1,200.00 laptop ($100/mo), running a batch Depreciation Run (12 entries, $5,865.29), and reviewing a $42,500.00 CIP Settlement.
    3. Controlling & SKF Allocation: Distributing $5,250.00 of shared IT overhead across operating cost centers and auditing results in Cost Center Performance.

    Part A: Real-Time Budget Control (AVC) Full Cycle

    Section titled “Part A: Real-Time Budget Control (AVC) Full Cycle”

    Step A1: Activate a Budget with Strict Hard-Stop AVC ($1,000.00)

    Section titled “Step A1: Activate a Budget with Strict Hard-Stop AVC ($1,000.00)”
    1. Verify Budgeting & AVC and Controlling are enabled in Accounting → More → Configuration → Settings.
    2. Open Accounting → More → Accounting → Budgets and click New:
      • Name: FY AVC Cycle Budget
      • Budget Control Profile: Strict Hard Stop (80% Warn / 100% Block)
      • Budget Line: Account 611000 Office Rent, Cost Center UM-QA-CC, Planned Amount: $1,000.00
    3. Click Confirm, then Approve & Activate AVC. The budget now shows Released = $1,000.00, Actual = $0.00, Available = $1,000.00.

    Step A2: Spend 85% and Trigger the Warning Band (BILL/2026/11/0023)

    Section titled “Step A2: Spend 85% and Trigger the Warning Band (BILL/2026/11/0023)”
    1. Open Accounting → Vendors → Bills → New for Baja Sentosa Abadi, set Bill Date, add a line on Account 611000 Office Rent tagged to Cost Center UM-QA-CC for $850.00, click Save, and Confirm (BILL/2026/11/0023).
    2. Reopen the Budget form:
      • Actual updates immediately to $850.00, Available drops to $150.00, and AVC Status switches to Warning (having crossed the 80% warning threshold).

    Step A3: Exceed 100% and Observe the Live AVC Hard-Stop Block

    Section titled “Step A3: Exceed 100% and Observe the Live AVC Hard-Stop Block”
    1. Draft a second Vendor Bill for $300.00 on the same Account 611000 Office Rent and Cost Center UM-QA-CC (cumulative spend would be $850 + $300 = $1,150.00, exceeding the $1,000.00 hard limit by $150.00).
    2. Click Confirm.
    3. ERPForAll blocks the posting immediately with an AVC error dialog stating exact figures:
      • Released 1000.00, Committed 0, Actual 850.00, Requested 300.00, Hard Limit 1000.00 (shortfall 150.00).
      • The bill stays safely in Draft—zero unauthorized expense hits the ledger.

    Step A4: Approve a Budget Amendment (+$500.00) and Post the Bill (BILL/2026/11/0024)

    Section titled “Step A4: Approve a Budget Amendment (+$500.00) and Post the Bill (BILL/2026/11/0024)”
    1. Open Accounting → More → Accounting → Budget Amendments and click New:
      • Select the target Budget, Process Type: Supplement (Increase Budget), select the 611000 Office Rent / UM-QA-CC line, and enter Net Change: +$500.00.
      • Click Confirm and Approve & Apply (Approved & Applied).
    2. Return to the $300.00 Draft Vendor Bill and click Confirm again. It now posts cleanly as BILL/2026/11/0024!
    3. Audit the updated budget and report:
      • Budget form: Original $1,000.00 + Revisions $500.00 = Released $1,500.00, Actual $1,150.00, Available $350.00, AVC Status = Within Budget.
      • Reporting → Budget Availability Control: Shows exact utilization 76.67% ($1,150 / $1,500) alongside company-wide budget totals.

    Part B: Fixed Asset Depreciation, Batch Runs & CIP Settlement Full Cycle

    Section titled “Part B: Fixed Asset Depreciation, Batch Runs & CIP Settlement Full Cycle”

    Step B1: Register an Asset and Post Period 1 Depreciation (GEN/2026/11/0005)

    Section titled “Step B1: Register an Asset and Post Period 1 Depreciation (GEN/2026/11/0005)”
    1. Open Accounting → More → Accounting → Assets & CIP → New:
      • Enter Name: Laptop Asset, Acquisition Value: $1,200.00, Method: Straight Line, Duration: 12 months ($100.00/mo), and assign Fixed Asset Account (151000), Accumulated Depreciation (151900), Depreciation Expense (681000), and Cost Center UM-QA-CC.
    2. Click Compute Depreciation (generating 12 monthly schedule rows of $100.00) and confirm to Running.
    3. Click Post Next Depreciation to post Period 1:
      • Period 1 switches to Posted and links to Journal Entry GEN/2026/11/0005 (Dr 681000 Depreciation of Equipment $100.00 [Cost Center UM-QA-CC] / Cr 151900 Accumulated Depreciation $100.00).
      • Asset summary updates automatically to Cumulative Depreciation = $100.00 and Net Book Value = $1,100.00.

    Step B2: Execute a Month-End Batch Depreciation Run

    Section titled “Step B2: Execute a Month-End Batch Depreciation Run”

    Instead of opening every company asset one by one at month-end:

    1. Open Accounting → More → Accounting → Depreciation Runs and click New.
    2. Enter the run name (for example, Nov 2026 Depreciation Run) and Cutoff Date (11/26/2026).
    3. Execute and post the run. ERPForAll collects every due depreciation line across all running assets in one batch—posting 12 entries totaling $5,865.29 and moving the run status to Posted.

    Step B3: Capitalize Construction in Progress via CIP Settlement

    Section titled “Step B3: Capitalize Construction in Progress via CIP Settlement”

    When an ongoing capital build (CIP asset) reaches commissioning:

    1. Open Accounting → More → Accounting → CIP Settlements (inspecting Conveyor section commissioned as a live benchmark).
    2. Select the source CIP Asset (Automated Packaging Line Expansion (CIP)), Settlement Date, and Mode (Partial Settlement or Full Settlement).
    3. Specify the receiver Fixed Asset (Conveyor section into service, Account 151000 Office Equipment, Cost Center Manufacturing Plant A) and settlement amount ($42,500.00).
    4. Post the settlement: ERPForAll generates Journal Entry GEN/2026/11/0002 transferring $42,500.00 from CIP into the depreciable Fixed Asset and marks the settlement Settled.
