    Sales orders: the order header and its operations

    Addon sale (depends on sales_team, product, mail and account), crate erp-addon-sale. It ships sale.order and its lines with prices, taxes and totals. The order is a thread model with activities. ADR 0199 governs the addon.

    Fields

    Section titled “Fields”
    FieldRule
    nameThe order number, drawn at creation (below). A name given at create is kept; after create no client write changes it.
    company_id, partner_idRequired. The company defaults to the current one.
    statedraft, sent, sale, cancel; changed only by the operations.
    lockedChanged only by action_lock and action_unlock.
    date_orderCreation time; set to the confirmation time by action_confirm.
    partner_invoice_id, partner_shipping_idStored, editable; computed from the customer’s invoice and delivery contacts (res.partner.address_get).
    user_idSalesperson, stored, editable: the customer’s salesperson (res.partner.user_id), else the commercial partner’s, else the current user when they are a salesperson. A saved order keeps its salesperson.
    team_idStored, editable: crm.team._get_default_team_id for the salesperson in the order’s company, the order’s present team preferred.
    client_order_ref, origin, commitment_date, validity_date, tag_ids, notePlain editable fields.

    Numbering

    Section titled “Numbering”

    An order created without a name, or with the placeholder New, takes the next number of the sequence record with code sale.order for its company: a record of that company wins over the shared one. The addon ships the shared record sale.seq_sale_order (prefix S, padding 5, as fields of the record, noupdate). The draw goes through erp_sdk::sequence::draw_by_code. Without a record for the company, the create is refused with a user error naming the code and the company; an order is never saved without a number.

    Operations

    Section titled “Operations”

    Each operation is an RPC method with effect = write that acts on the whole record set. It locks the rows in id order, reads the states, refuses the whole call when one order is not in a legal source state (the error names the orders and their states), and writes through the base write.

    OperationFromTo
    action_quotation_sentdraftsent
    action_confirmdraft, sentsale, then locked when the setting is on
    action_canceldraft, sent, sale, not lockedcancel
    action_draftsent, canceldraft
    action_locksalelocked; Sales administrators only
    action_unlocklockedunlocked; Sales administrators only
    deletedraft, cancelremoved

    Cancel, lock and unlock succeed without writing when the order is already in their end state.

    Client writes

    Section titled “Client writes”

    write refuses state, locked, invoicing_closed, invoice_status, delivery_status and name in every state, also with sudo (create refuses the same fields except name, and refuses them as default_<field> context keys too), any field of a cancelled order, the customer of a confirmed order, the company of a confirmed order and of a quotation that has lines (a quotation without lines may change company), and the protected fields of a locked order (order_line among them). All are validation errors that name the field or the order. A create without invoice or delivery address takes the customer’s (address_get) before the INSERT, since both fields are required.

    Seams for satellite addons

    Section titled “Seams for satellite addons”

    Override these with #[method(override)] and call sup once (ADR 0199 D4):

    MethodResult
    _confirmation_error_messageOption<String>: the first refusal of a confirmation
    _prepare_confirmation_valuesVals<SaleOrder>: what a confirmation writes; add values through field handles
    _action_confirm(): the step where satellites act; empty in sale
    _should_be_lockedRecords: the orders a confirmation locks
    _action_cancel(): the cancel step; writes state = cancel in sale
    _get_protected_fieldsProtectedFields<SaleOrder>: the header fields a locked order refuses

    ProtectedFields::with takes a field handle of the order or of an extension of it, so a field that does not exist cannot be named.

    Order lines

    Section titled “Order lines”

    sale.order.line (company rule by its company and through its order; the Sales administrator has full access). A line is a product line (display_type empty) or a section, subsection or note line. Database checks: a product line has a unit; a section or note line has no product, unit, price, quantity or lead time (create clears them); the lead time is zero or more.

    FieldRule
    product_id, nameThe description is the product’s name unless written.
    product_uom_qty, product_uom_idQuantity (default 1) and unit (the product’s unit unless written).
    allowed_uom_idsThe product’s units (product.product._get_available_uoms): its unit, plus its other units when several units are enabled.
    price_unitFrom the order’s pricelist, else the product’s sales price; editable (see below).
    discount, tax_idsDiscount in percent; the taxes (see below).
    price_subtotal, price_tax, price_totalComputed amounts in the order currency; never written by a client.
    qty_delivered_method, qty_deliveredmanual in sale: the delivered quantity is entered by hand.
    invoice_policyThe product’s invoice_policy, else the company’s sale_invoice_policy.
    qty_invoiced, qty_to_invoiceInvoiced quantity from the hook (zero until invoices exist); to invoice is the ordered or delivered quantity less the invoiced one, on a confirmed order only.
    invoice_statusno, to invoice, upselling or invoiced.

    On the order: order_line, invoicing_closed (action_close_invoicing on confirmed orders, action_reopen_order), invoice_status and delivery_status (over goods lines: pending, partial, full).

    The derived line fields (state, company_id, order_partner_id, salesman_id, is_downpayment, qty_delivered_method, qty_invoiced, qty_to_invoice, invoice_status) are refused on create and write for every caller, also with sudo, through the order’s order_line commands and as default_<field> context keys. A line is not added to a cancelled or locked order, not changed on a cancelled one, and not deleted from either. A line never moves to another order: a write of order_id naming another order, directly or through another order’s order_line link or set commands, is refused with a user error for every caller. A product line created without description or unit takes the product’s display name and unit before the INSERT, and a product created without invoice_policy gets the current company’s policy as its default.

    Write guards, in order: the kind of a line does not change (a subsection may become a section); the product does not change on a down payment, a cancelled order, or a confirmed order that is locked or has delivered or invoiced quantities; a new quantity on a confirmed order calls _update_line_quantity; a locked order refuses the fields of _get_protected_fields (product, description, unit price, unit, quantity, discount, taxes, order), except a description change on down payments only. A product line of a confirmed order is not deleted (_check_line_unlink).

    Seams of the line (override, call sup once):

    MethodResult
    _get_consu_qty_delivered_methodBTreeMap<RecId, String>: the method of each goods line
    _prepare_qty_deliveredBTreeMap<RecId, Decimal>: the delivered quantity of the non-manual lines
    _prepare_qty_invoicedBTreeMap<RecId, Decimal>: the invoiced quantity of each line
    _can_be_invoiced_aloneRecords: the lines that may be invoiced without the others
    _get_protected_fieldsProtectedFields<SaleOrderLine>
    _update_line_quantity(), with the new quantity
    _check_line_unlinkRecords: the lines that may not be deleted

    The order’s delivery_status and invoice_status are computes that sale_stock and the invoicing addons override with #[compute(override)].

    Prices, taxes and totals

    Section titled “Prices, taxes and totals”

    On the order: pricelist_id (none: the products’ sales prices; not changed once confirmed), currency_id (the pricelist’s, else the company’s), currency_rate (company currency to order currency on the order day, latest rate strictly before it), fiscal_position_id (computed from the customer and delivery address with account.fiscal.position._get_fiscal_position in the order’s company, editable), and amount_untaxed, amount_tax, amount_total.

    • Price. A new product line without price_unit, or with a price_unit equal to the technical_price_unit sent with it (the line form sends its untouched default price 0 with the hidden technical price 0), takes the pricelist price (ProductPricelist::get_product_price at the order date), else the product’s lst_price converted from the company currency, in the line’s unit and rounded to “Product Price”. A pricelist in another currency than the company’s prices only through a fixed-price rule (ProductPricelist::get_product_rule); any other price would need a conversion inside the pricelist, which is not built, and is refused by name (a typed price is accepted). The price the line was priced at is kept in technical_price_unit; a typed price keeps the technical price sent with it, so it counts as set by hand. A later change of product, unit or quantity prices the line again unless the same write gives a price, its price differs from technical_price_unit (set by hand), it has an invoiced quantity, or it is a down payment. A pricelist change within one currency keeps hand-set prices and prices the other lines again; on a quotation with lines, a pricelist in another currency is refused by name (there is no update-prices operation yet). The rule’s minimum quantity is read in the product’s unit, the line’s quantity rounded at the Product Unit precision.
    • The form. A new quotation starts with the active company (default_get) and a new line does not need a typed description: it takes the product’s (an empty or null description counts as not typed). A new line created without tax_ids takes the product’s taxes; a given set, the empty one too, is kept (copies and API calls included). After confirmation date_order, validity_date and fiscal_position_id are refused on write, and on a locked order user_id and team_id, as the form shows them read-only.
    • Taxes. Not given: the product’s taxes_id of the order company or a parent; for a line without product and display type, the company’s account_sale_tax_id; then mapped with the order’s fiscal position (map_tax). A new product takes its taxes again unless they are given.
    • Amounts. erp_addon_account::tax::compute on price, quantity, discount and taxes, rounded per line in the order currency; the order sums its lines. A group of taxes and a reverse-charge tax are refused by name, as on invoices; the company’s “round globally” choice is not applied yet.
    • Derived fields. amount_*, currency_id and currency_rate of the order, and price_subtotal, price_tax, price_total, technical_price_unit and currency_id of a line, are refused on create and write, also as default_<field> context keys and inside order_line commands; technical_price_unit is accepted on a line create only, where it tells an untouched price from a typed one (an untouched price is replaced by the computed one, a typed price keeps it, so the line counts as priced by hand).

    Screens, menus and the Sales application

    Section titled “Screens, menus and the Sales application”

    sale ships the views in views/sale_order_views.xml: the search view view_sales_order_filter, the lists view_quotation_tree and view_order_tree, the form view_order_form, and the default list and form of sale.order.line (sale_order_line_view_list, sale_order_line_view_form), which the form’s order_line field shows; a line opens in its form as a dialog. The actions sale.action_quotations and sale.action_orders have no domain and no context. The menus of views/sale_menus.xml: sale_menu_root (Sales, salespeople) with sale_order_menu (Quotations, Orders, Customers) and menu_sale_config (Sales administrators: Sales Teams).

    The root menu ships inactive. The data-only addon sale_management (depends on sale, no crate) is the Sales application: its data file sets sale.sale_menu_root active. Uninstalling it leaves the menu active while sale stays installed: a data-only addon cannot undo its data, and an uninstall hook would have to be linked into every binary that reads the addon folder. Extend a Sales screen with an inheriting view of these ids; add a menu under sale.sale_order_menu or sale.menu_sale_config. What each screen does and does not do yet: docs/design/sale-orders-views-matrix.md.

    Access

    Section titled “Access”

    The rows of security/ir.access.csv (reference odoo/addons/sale/security/ir.access.csv:32-41):

    Groupsale.ordersale.order.line
    sales_team.group_sale_salesmancru on own and unassigned orderscrud on lines of own and unassigned orders
    sales_team.group_sale_salesman_all_leadscru on every ordercrud on every line
    sales_team.group_sale_managercrudcrud

    “Own and unassigned” is the domain ['|', ('user_id', '=', user.id), ('user_id', '=', False)] on the order and the same over the stored salesman_id on the line. The company rule of each model is generated from its company_access declaration: an order by its company_id (exact, no empty company), a line by its own stored company_id (the order’s) and through its order. The Sales administrator also creates and changes partners, and manages pricelists, pricelist rules and activity types.

    The five feature groups (sale.group_auto_done_setting, group_discount_per_so_line, group_warning_sale, group_proforma_sales, group_services_and_material) are light feature groups (res.groups._get_light_group_xmlids, extended in src/res_groups.rs): no implied group, no access row, held by nobody until a setting switches them on. The lock is the declared setting sale.lock_confirmed_sales; the other four switches come with the Sales settings page. A satellite addon that adds a Sales role declares its own order and line rows.

    Not built yet

    Section titled “Not built yet”

    Chatter tracking of the salesperson and team (tracking of recomputed fields is not built). The partner’s own pricelist and fiscal position defaults beyond _get_fiscal_position, the discount computed from a pricelist rule, currency conversion inside a pricelist; the invoice link (cancelling draft invoices with the order); the signature fields; default search filters of the two actions.
